# Pricing & Monetization Model (Draft)

## Placement Tiers
| Tier | Description | Base Price (USD) | Duration Cap | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spotlight Banner | Hero row spanning width, animated reveal | $18–$30 / day | 3–5 days | Premium upsell, includes analytics + sponsor badge |
| Prime Quadrant | Large tile upper grid | $10–$15 / day | 7 days | Includes impression estimates + optional CTA button |
| Community Tile | Standard tile rotating every few minutes | Free–$3 / 12 hrs | 1–2 days | Always keep a free allotment to encourage participation |

## Dynamic Pricing Hooks
- Peak-hour multiplier (e.g., +25% between 4–8pm local).
- Surge pricing when occupancy > 80% for a given board.
- Discount bundles for multi-day or recurring bookings.
- Promotional codes and referral credits redeemable at checkout.

## Revenue Share
- Platform fee: 12% baseline, flexible per board.
- Referral bonus: 5% fee credit for board owners when invitees purchase.
- Spotlight sponsorship: negotiated rev-share, custom backgrounds/branding.

## Upsell Moments
1. **After first free post:** prompt to claim profile and unlock editing/history.
2. **Board owners hitting occupancy goals:** suggest raising prices or enabling surge.
3. **Inactive boards:** offer promo boost (temporary discount) to drive activity.
4. **Audience insights:** sell add-on analytics pack (impressions, CTR, conversions).

## Next Validation Steps
- Gather willingness-to-pay estimates via user interviews + fake door tests.
- Run price sensitivity survey targeting board owners and advertisers.
- Instrument conversion funnel to observe churn points pre-payment.

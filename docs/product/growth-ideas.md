# Growth & Revenue Tweaks (Quick Wins)

1. **Board Spotlight Rotation** – Charge a premium for the rotating hero slot and surface it on the landing page alongside success metrics.
2. **Creator Referral Credits** – Provide $10 credit for both inviter and invitee when a paid placement closes; auto-apply credits at checkout.
3. **Claim Prompt Nudges** – After two anonymous posts, surface a modal offering analytics and edit controls in exchange for sign-up.
4. **Targeted Follow-up Emails** – Capture optional email after free post; drip campaign showcasing premium placements, success stories, and referral program.
5. **Sponsored Themes** – Let brands skin entire boards (background, palette) for a fixed fee while preserving user-generated content.
6. **Analytics Snapshot Paywall** – Offer heatmap and engagement stats as an add-on for advertisers wanting proof of reach.
7. **Board Owner Leaderboard** – Showcase top-earning boards publicly to encourage competition and inspire new owners.
8. **Limited-Time Launch Bundles** – Discount packages that include spotlight slots + social media amplification for the first 30 days post-launch.
